# Threat Assessment

Name the service, business outcome, users, data classification, trust boundaries, deployment assumptions and excluded areas. Link the relevant architecture view and requirements.

Consider identity spoofing, unauthorised change, information disclosure, loss of evidence, service disruption, privilege abuse and missed detection. Use system specific threats, not a checklist score.

## Verification

Map each material treatment to a negative or positive test, log event, recovery exercise, review or independent assessment. State the evidence retained and the role that accepts residual risk.

## Review Triggers

Include change to data sensitivity, users, external access, trust boundary, supplier, identity provider, export route, retention or recovery requirement.
